Medical Programs We Help You Apply For

China has a large network of medical universities and teaching hospitals. Many offer English-medium programs for international students. We help you shortlist universities according to your budget and goals.

MBBS

The core medical degree, generally around 6 years including clinical internship, at universities offering English-medium programs.

BDS (Dentistry)

A dental surgery degree for students interested in building a professional career in dentistry.

MD and Master's

Postgraduate medical study for qualified doctors interested in specialisation and research.

Nursing and Pharmacy

Undergraduate and postgraduate opportunities in nursing, pharmacy and allied health sciences.

Why Students Choose China

Parents and students often ask whether China is a sensible choice for medical education. Here are some important factors to consider.

Affordable Education Tuition and living costs can be lower than in many Western countries.
Hospital Training Medical programs include clinical training at teaching hospitals.
English-Medium Programs Many universities offer programs taught in English for international students.
Modern Facilities Universities offer student facilities, accommodation and transport options.

Always verify that your chosen university and degree meet the medical registration requirements of the country where you intend to practise.
