Programs we help you apply for

China has a large network of medical universities and teaching hospitals, and many offer English-medium programs for international students. I will help you shortlist universities that match your budget and goals.

MBBS

The core medical degree. Usually about 6 years including clinical internship, taught in English at many universities.

BDS (Dentistry)

A bachelor's degree in dental surgery for students who want a career in dentistry.

MD and Master's

Postgraduate medical study for doctors who want to specialise or move into research.

Nursing, Pharmacy and other health degrees

Undergraduate and postgraduate options across allied health fields.

Why students choose China

Parents and students often ask me whether China is a sensible choice. In my experience, these are the reasons that matter most.

Lower total costTuition and living costs are generally lower than in many Western countries.
Hospital trainingPrograms include clinical rotations in large teaching hospitals.
English-mediumMany programs are taught in English for international students.
Safe, modern citiesGood transport, hostels and student facilities.

Before you apply, always confirm that your chosen university is approved by the medical council of the country where you plan to practise. I will walk you through how to check this.
